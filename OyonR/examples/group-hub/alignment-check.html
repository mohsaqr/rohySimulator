Clock alignment check

Open this page on every laptop, point it at the same hub, and press Sync. Each screen then flashes white for 100 ms at every whole group-time second that is a multiple of the period. Film all screens with one camera (≥ 60 fps): flashes that start within 6 frames of each other (100 ms) pass. Markers broadcast from the hub (POST /groups/:id/marker) are logged with their local receipt time as the fallback estimate.
