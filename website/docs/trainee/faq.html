Common questions and fixes when running a case. If something here does not match what you see, ask your instructor.

Sessions and progress

Did I lose my session when I refreshed?

No. Refreshing the page or closing and reopening the tab does not end your session. The simulator restores your case, your room and your work when you come back. A session ends only when you click End & Debrief, load a different case, or your instructor ends it. If a case still looks empty after a refresh, check you are signed in as the same user.

I clicked End & Debrief by mistake. Can I reopen the case?

No. Ending a case is final: the timeline stops and chat, exams and orders lock. You can still review the transcript in the debrief, but the case cannot be reopened. Start a fresh case to practise again.

"This session is open in another browser tab"

You have the same session open in more than one tab. The last tab to write wins, which can overwrite chat history. Work in one tab at a time; dismiss the banner once you have closed the extra tab.

How do I switch to a different case?

Open Settings (top-left of the patient screen) → Open Settings → Cases tab, and open the case you want. Loading a new case ends the current session and clears its chat. Finish or End & Debrief first if you want it kept cleanly.

Results

My lab or imaging results are not appearing

Most results have a turnaround time and are not instant. In the Laboratory or Radiology room the worklist shows each order as Pending with a countdown, then Ready. The worklist updates itself every few seconds, and a count badge appears on the room's button in the bottom bar when something is ready, so a refresh is unnecessary. An order that still reads Pending has a result that is on its way. If an order stays pending, tell your instructor: the case may have no result configured for it.

I opened a result but it is gone from the centre

Opening a result marks it Viewed. Opened reports become pills above the viewer; click the pill to reopen it, or click its row again in the worklist on the right. Removing a pill with its X closes the report and keeps the result: the worklist row stays.

Voice and audio

I have no audio / the patient does not speak

Check, in order:

  1. Is voice mode on? Click Voice at the top-right of the chat panel so it reads Voice on. If there is no Voice button, your instructor has not enabled voice and you should work in text.
  2. Browser and origin. Speech needs Chrome or Edge over HTTPS.
  3. System volume / output device. Confirm the browser tab is not muted.
  4. If the simulator shows "No voice configured", the case or patient persona has no voice set. Your instructor fixes that; continue in text mode.

The microphone will not start

The simulator shows the exact reason. Usual fixes: allow microphone access for the site, serve the page over HTTPS, plug in a microphone, or speak closer to it. See Voice mode for the full table.

Alarms

An alarm keeps going off after I acknowledged it

Acknowledging silences the alert and leaves the cause in place. The vital stays out of range until you treat the underlying problem, and a silenced-but-still-abnormal alarm stays listed so you do not forget it. See Vitals & alarms.

My old alarms are silent in a new case

Acknowledgements and snoozes belong to the case you set them in. Starting a different case clears them, so new alarms in a new patient are not accidentally silenced.

Other characters

A nurse / relative / consultant tab is disabled

Some characters must be summoned before they can talk. Use the Call button on their tab; the message box stays disabled with a "not available" note until they arrive. See Taking a history.

Still stuck?

If a problem is not listed here, or the simulator is behaving in a way these pages do not describe, contact your instructor or platform administrator. Your session activity is recorded, which helps them investigate.
