Run your first virtual-patient case in about ten minutes. This page takes you from the login screen to a live session with a patient who answers your questions and a monitor that responds to what you do.

Training only

The simulated patient is not real and the simulator does not provide medical advice. Nothing you do here affects a real person, and nothing here is clinical guidance. See the glossary for how terms are used across this documentation.

Before you start

You need:

  • A user account (username and password). If you do not have one, use Create Account on the sign-in screen, or ask your Teacher for credentials.
  • A modern browser. Chrome or Edge are recommended, especially if you plan to use voice mode.
  • The site served over HTTPS if you want to talk to the patient with your microphone. Speech recognition starts only on a secure origin.

1. Sign in

  1. Open the simulator. You land on the Sign In screen.
  2. Enter your Username and Password.
  3. Click Sign In.

If sign-in fails, the screen shows the reason in a red banner. Check the username and password, then try again. To make a new account, click Create Account and follow the register form.

2. Pick a case

A case is one clinical scenario: a patient with a presentation, labs, imaging, exam findings and treatments behind it.

  • If your instructor set a default case, the simulator loads it automatically and you can start talking right away.
  • To choose a different case, open Settings (top-left of the patient screen) → Open Settings, go to the Cases tab, and open the case you want. Loading a case returns you to the patient screen with that patient ready.

The screen header withholds the diagnosis. You work it out.

3. Start the session

There is no separate "Start" button. Your first message to the patient starts the session. The chat panel is on the lower-left of the screen.

  1. Type a question in the message box, for example Hello, what brought you in today?
  2. Press Enter or click the send button.

The patient replies, the session begins, and the monitor on the right starts running the patient's vitals. From this point everything you do is part of one session: questions, exams, orders and treatments.

4. Work the case

You now work across rooms, switched from the bar along the bottom of the screen. Every install carries these:

Room What you do there
Patient Take the history by chatting with the patient
Examination Perform a physical exam on a body map
Laboratory Order and read blood and other lab tests
Radiology Order and read imaging
Consultant Debrief and reflect after you end the case

The 3D Room, 12-lead ECG, Pathology and PACS rooms join the bar when the case includes that material. See the rooms for how moving between them works. Order medications from the Treatments button that floats above the bottom bar on the patient screen.

5. End and debrief

When you are finished:

  1. On the patient screen, click End & Debrief (top-right).
  2. Confirm in the dialog. Ending the case stops the patient's timeline and locks orders, exams and chat.
  3. You land in the Consultant room to review and reflect. You cannot reopen an ended case.

Refresh is safe

Refreshing the page or closing the tab does not end your session. The simulator restores your case, your room and your work when you come back. A session ends only when you click End & Debrief, switch to a different case, or your instructor ends it. See the FAQ if a session looks lost.
