A formal statement of what Rohy is and is not. Compliance owners should treat this page as the scope boundary of the platform.

Warning

Rohy is a medical-education and clinical-simulation training tool. It is not a medical device. It must not be used in the care of, or for decisions about, real patients.

Scope

Rohy presents virtual patients — synthetic, authored scenarios with simulated vitals, simulated laboratory and radiology results, and an AI-driven patient persona. Every clinical signal a trainee sees is generated by the scenario engine and authored case content. Nothing in the system observes, measures, diagnoses, or treats a real human being.

The platform exists to let learners practice history-taking, examination, ordering, interpretation, and management in a safe, repeatable, instrumented environment, and to let educators author scenarios and review learner activity.

Not a medical device

Rohy:

  • is not cleared, approved, certified, or registered as a medical device by any regulatory body;
  • performs no diagnosis, triage, or treatment recommendation for real patients;
  • produces outputs (vitals, lab values, AI patient responses, any emotion-capture aggregates) that are simulated or research signals, not clinical findings;
  • makes no automated decision about a real learner's competence, progression, or grade derived from emotion capture (see Oyon & EU AI Act).

No real patient data

Important

Do not enter real patient identifiable information, real protected health information, or any data from an actual clinical encounter into Rohy. The platform is built for synthetic scenario content. It carries no HIPAA, GDPR-clinical, or medical-device compliance certification, and the deploying organization is responsible for ensuring only simulated data is used.

Cases, scenarios, and patient personas are authored teaching content. Any resemblance to a real individual should be removed before a case is published.

Compliance posture, not certification

Rohy implements security and data-handling controls — role-based access (RBAC), a tamper-evident audit chain, centralized response redaction, and data retention with purge. These support an organization's own compliance work; they are not a substitute for it and do not constitute certification. Rohy holds no FIPS, HIPAA, or medical-device attestation. Where this documentation describes alignment with a regulation, it describes posture and design intent, not certified compliance.

Responsibility of the deploying organization

The organization deploying Rohy is responsible for:

  • restricting use to training with synthetic data only;
  • obtaining any legal/ethics sign-off required for optional features (in particular Oyon emotion capture under EU AI Act Art. 5 — see Oyon & EU AI Act);
  • production hardening (see the Hardening checklist);
  • communicating to learners and educators that all clinical content is simulated.
